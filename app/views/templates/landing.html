A stream is hours long. The best part is seconds. Clip Studio finds it, makes the video and posts it for you.

1 · Find

Pick a game, or a creator.

Choose a game and the studio searches Twitch and YouTube for fresh, popular clips. Or type the name of one creator and take clips only from them.

  • Every clip credits its creator and links the original
  • Clips you use are yours alone: nobody else gets the same one
  • Region and safety checks run on every clip

2 · Make

From clip to finished video.

The studio cuts the moment into a vertical Short or joins many into a long compilation, adds an AI voice and burned-in captions, and writes the title, description and tags.

  • Choose AI commentary over music, or keep the original sound
  • Write the title or commentary yourself to save tokens
  • A content-safety check always runs first
3 · Look

Thumbnails that don't all look the same.

Eight styles, five bold fonts, your own colours and logo. Draw one AI picture or three to choose from, pick the exact moment, and edit the text in your browser.

  • Compare three versions side by side
  • A full in-browser editor for the final touch
